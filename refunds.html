If the Software doesn’t work for you, you may request a full refund within 3 days of purchase — or within any longer period your local law requires. Just contact us at muselik+desco@gmail.com with your order details, or use the refund option in the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy.

Once a purchase is refunded or charged back, the license ends and the associated license key is deactivated. The next time Desco re-checks the license online (about every 14 days, when it launches), the paid features stop working and the app returns to its free tier. Any free desktops you unlocked over time are kept. Activation codes we issue directly work offline and are not re-checked; if such a purchase is refunded, the license still ends under our Terms & License.

Because Desco has a free version you can try first, we keep the voluntary window short — but your statutory rights below always apply.

How refunds are processed

Purchases are handled by our Merchant of Record, Lemon Squeezy, which is the seller of record: your purchase contract, including refunds and withdrawal, is with Lemon Squeezy under its Buyer Terms. Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method by Lemon Squeezy; the time to appear depends on your bank or card provider.

Your statutory rights

If you are a consumer in the EU/EEA, UK, or another region with a statutory right of withdrawal for digital purchases, that right applies in addition to this policy. Note that where you expressly consent to immediate delivery of digital content at checkout and acknowledge that your withdrawal right is thereby waived, the statutory cooling-off period may not apply — but our own guarantee above still does.
