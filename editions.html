Editions & Capabilities

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Desco is one app with three unlock stages, not separate paid/free editions. Everyone starts free, earns more desktops over time, and can buy the full version whenever they want the rest immediately.

Desco's unlock stages

Included at every stage: click-to-switch and switch-by-typing-a-name, jumping back to your previous desktop, the Desktop Map (2D per-display switcher), the desktop comparison report, multi-display support, custom keyboard shortcuts, and free auto-updates.

macOS itself supports up to 16 desktops per display, switched with ⌃-number (1–10) and ⌃⌥-number (11–16); a fullscreen app keeps its own name rather than a Desco desktop name. These limits apply at every stage.
